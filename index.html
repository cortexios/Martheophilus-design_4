OUR STORY

A Patron who wanted Pune to learn.

Late Archbishop Dr. Philipose Mar Theophilus believed in building schools on firm legal and charitable ground. He registered trusts to promote education and relief work, and the Malankara Orthodox Church Council of Bombay grew from them into a network of schools and junior colleges.

1989He started St. Thomas Public School in Kirkee, Pune, with a pre-school in an unfinished church building.
His wishHe often spoke of seeing Pune become an educational hub.

School Policy

Code of Conduct

Our code of conduct reflects the values, traditions and expectations that shape every student's journey at Dr. Mar Theophilus School.

At Dr. Mar Theophilus School, we believe that a strong moral framework is as important as academic achievement. Our Code of Conduct has been developed to ensure that every student, teacher and parent contributes to a safe, inclusive and inspiring school community.

These guidelines apply to all students from Nursery to Grade 10 and cover behaviour within school premises, during school events, on school transport and in any context where a student represents the school.

General Behaviour Expectations

  • Students must be respectful, polite and courteous to all staff, peers and visitors at all times.
  • Use of abusive, offensive or discriminatory language is strictly prohibited.
  • Students must not engage in any form of bullying, physical altercation or harassment.
  • Mobile phones and electronic devices must not be used during school hours without teacher permission.
  • Students are expected to maintain decorum in classrooms, corridors, the library and all school spaces.

Dress Code & Uniform

  • The prescribed school uniform must be worn on all school days and at all official events.
  • Uniforms must be clean, well-pressed and properly worn at all times.
  • Hair must be neatly kept; boys should have short, tidy hair; girls may tie long hair with a black or navy band.
  • Jewellery (other than small stud earrings for girls) is not permitted during school hours.
  • Footwear must conform to the school's prescribed style â€” black closed shoes with black socks.

Academic Integrity

  • Plagiarism, copying or any form of academic dishonesty during tests or assignments is a serious offence.
  • Students must submit their own original work for all assessments and projects.
  • Tampering with school records, marks or official documents will result in strict disciplinary action.
  • All home assignments and classwork must be completed on time and submitted as instructed.

Care of School Property

  • Students are responsible for the care and maintenance of all school property, furniture and equipment.
  • Any damage caused must be reported immediately; the cost of repairs may be borne by the student concerned.
  • Writing on walls, desks, chairs or library books is strictly forbidden.
  • Littering is not permitted anywhere on school premises â€” use designated bins at all times.

Prohibited Items & Activities

  • Tobacco, alcohol, drugs or any banned substances are strictly prohibited on school premises.
  • Weapons, sharp objects or any item that could cause harm to others must not be brought to school.
  • Gambling, betting or any unlawful activity on school premises is a serious disciplinary offence.
  • Unauthorised collection of money from students or parents is not allowed.

"Discipline is not a punishment â€” it is the bridge between who you are and who you want to become. At Dr. Mar Theophilus School, we nurture the habits that build exceptional human beings."

â€” School Administration
Consequences

Violations & Consequences

Violations of the Code of Conduct are addressed in a fair, consistent and age-appropriate manner. The school follows a graduated response framework:

Verbal Warning

First-time minor infractions are addressed through a private conversation with the class teacher, allowing the student to understand and correct their behaviour.

Written Notice

Repeated minor infractions or a single moderate offence may result in a written notice that must be acknowledged and countersigned by the parent or guardian.

Principal's Office

Serious misconduct is referred to the Principal's office for a formal counselling session and a discussion with parents regarding corrective action.

Suspension / Expulsion

Severe or repeated violations may result in temporary suspension or, in extreme cases, expulsion from the school â€” a decision taken by the management after due process.

Discipline & Behaviour Framework

We believe discipline is a life skill â€” one that empowers students to make responsible choices inside and outside the classroom.

The discipline framework at Dr. Mar Theophilus School is grounded in the belief that every student deserves a safe, respectful learning environment. Our approach emphasises positive reinforcement, clear expectations and restorative practices rather than purely punitive measures.

Parents and guardians are considered essential partners in this process. We actively encourage open communication between home and school to ensure consistency in guiding students towards responsible, ethical behaviour.

Framework

Three-Tier Discipline Model

1

Preventive

Clear rules, regular orientation for students and parents, and a positive school culture that makes good choices the natural default.

2

Corrective

When issues arise, teachers address them through counselling, restorative conversations and parent communication before escalation.

3

Consequential

Persistent or serious misconduct leads to formal consequences ranging from written notice to suspension, as determined by the Principal.

Anti-Bullying Commitment

Dr. Mar Theophilus School has a zero-tolerance policy towards all forms of bullying â€” physical, verbal, social or cyber. Any reported or witnessed incident of bullying is investigated promptly and addressed with appropriate intervention, counselling and parental involvement. Students who experience bullying are encouraged to report it to any trusted staff member without fear of retaliation.

Restorative Practices

Where appropriate, the school employs restorative practices â€” structured conversations that help students understand the impact of their actions, take accountability, and make amends. This approach is particularly effective for interpersonal conflicts and fosters empathy, communication skills and long-term behaviour change rather than simply applying punitive measures.

Role of Parents

  • Attend parent-teacher meetings and respond promptly to school communications regarding their child's behaviour.
  • Reinforce the school's expectations and values at home to ensure a consistent message.
  • Avoid encouraging or condoning behaviour at home that contradicts school policy.
  • Contact the class teacher or school counsellor if they notice changes in their child's behaviour or attitude.

Examination Rules & Assessment Pattern

Fair, transparent and consistent evaluation that reflects each student's true potential and encourages continuous improvement.

The school follows the CBSE examination and assessment framework, adapted where appropriate for the foundational and preparatory stages. Clear rules and advance notice of examination schedules ensure that every student can prepare effectively and perform to their best ability.

Pattern

Assessment Structure by Stage

Foundational & Preparatory (Nursery â€“ Grade V)

Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) through class participation, oral assessments, projects and periodic written tests. No high-stakes terminal examinations at this stage â€” progress is communicated through report cards twice a year.

Middle School (Grade VI â€“ VIII)

Two terms per academic year. Each term includes a Periodic Assessment (PA), a Half-Yearly Examination, and an Annual Examination. Internal assessments include notebooks, subject enrichment activities and projects.

Rules

Examination Rules

Admit Card

Students must carry their school identity card and admit card (where applicable) to every examination. Entry may be denied without valid identification.

No Malpractice

Any form of cheating, copying or possession of unauthorised material during an examination will result in immediate cancellation of that paper and disciplinary action.

Punctuality

Students must report to the examination hall at least 10 minutes before the scheduled start time. Entry is not permitted after the first 15 minutes.

No Devices

Mobile phones, smartwatches and electronic devices of any kind are strictly prohibited in the examination hall. Violation will be treated as malpractice.

Permission Required

Students who need to leave the hall during the examination must obtain explicit permission from the invigilator and will be accompanied by a staff member.

Medical Absence

A medical certificate from a registered doctor must be submitted within three working days of an absence from an examination for a re-test to be considered.

Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Over three decades of shaping young minds, building character and nurturing the leaders of tomorrow.

Dr. Mar Theophilus School stands as a testament to the enduring power of faith-based, values-driven education in the heart of Dhanori, Pune. Founded under the auspices of the Mar Theophilus Educational Society, the school has grown from a modest institution into one of Pune's most respected CBSE-affiliated schools, serving thousands of students across the Nursery to Grade X spectrum.

The school's story is one of quiet determination and remarkable growth. From its earliest days in Dhanori, it has remained steadfast in its founding mission: to provide high-quality, affordable education rooted in Christian values of compassion, integrity and service. That mission continues to guide every decision the school makes.

Milestones

Our Journey Through the Years

Est.

Foundation

Dr. Mar Theophilus School was established by the Mar Theophilus Educational Society with a vision to provide quality education to the children of Dhanori and surrounding areas of Pune. The school began with a small number of students in the primary section.

CBSE

CBSE Affiliation

A landmark achievement: the school received formal affiliation from the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi. This opened new doors for students and established the school as a serious academic institution in Pune.

Sec.

Secondary Section Expansion

The school expanded to include the secondary section, offering Classes IX and X. The first batch appeared for the CBSE Class X Board Examination, setting a tradition of strong results that continues to this day.

Infra

Infrastructure Development

Major improvements were undertaken: new classrooms, a science laboratory, a library and a computer centre were added. Sports facilities were expanded to include a multi-purpose court.

Now

Today

Today, Dr. Mar Theophilus School stands as a thriving community of learners, educators and families. With over 1,200 students, a dedicated faculty and a rich tradition of excellence, the school continues to grow guided by its founding values.

Overview

Where Foundations Become Futures

The High School years are among the most formative in a student's academic journey. At Dr. Mar Theophilus School, Classes 8 to 10 are designed to strengthen subject knowledge, cultivate independent learning habits, and build the confidence students need to excel in board examinations and beyond. Our teachers combine rigorous academic content with individual guidance, ensuring that every student not only meets curriculum expectations but develops the skills, values and self-belief to pursue their chosen future with purpose.

Class 8

Age 13 – 14 years

A bridge year — extending Middle School learning into deeper subject content. Students build analytical skills, begin structured project work and develop stronger study routines.

Class 9

Age 14 – 15 years

Full board-level curriculum begins. Emphasis on concept clarity, practice and time management. Students explore subject specialisations and begin focusing on their examination strategy.

Class 10

Age 15 – 16 years

The board examination year. Intensive revision, mock tests, individual mentoring and career guidance ensure students are confident, prepared and ready for the next stage of their education.

Curriculum

What Students Learn

A comprehensive curriculum that builds deep subject mastery alongside vital life skills.

Mathematics

Advanced algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics — logical reasoning and structured problem-solving.

Science

Physics, Chemistry and Biology explored through concept clarity, experiments and practical learning.

Languages

English, Marathi and Hindi — literature, grammar, composition, communication and language proficiency.

Social Science

History, Geography, Civics and Economics — developing awareness of society, the nation and the world.

Computer Education

Digital literacy, programming fundamentals, data handling and responsible technology use.

Life Skills

Critical thinking, time management, decision-making, teamwork and emotional intelligence.

Learning Approach

How We Teach

Our High School classrooms blend academic rigour with practical application and personal support.

Concept-based Learning

Deep understanding over rote memorisation — students learn to apply knowledge, not just recall it.

Interactive Classrooms

Discussions, Q&A, peer teaching and case-based reasoning keep learning active and engaging.

Practical Activities

Lab experiments, research projects and real-world application connect theory to lived experience.

Assessments & Feedback

Regular tests, detailed feedback and progress reviews help students identify strengths and gaps early.

Study Habit Guidance

Teachers coach students on planning, revision strategies and maintaining focus through the academic year.

Individual Attention

One-on-one academic support, mentoring and career guidance tailored to each student's needs.

Academic Preparation

Prepared for Success

Our structured preparation programme gives every student the best possible platform for board examinations and future academic pathways.

Strong Subject Foundations

Classes 8–10 are sequenced to build progressively — each year reinforces and extends the last.

Structured Exam Preparation

Syllabus-mapped study plans, chapter-wise tests and full-length practice papers from Class 9 onwards.

Practice Tests & Assessments

Regular unit tests, periodic exams and mock board papers under timed, exam-like conditions.

Study Planning & Time Management

Guided study timetables, revision schedules and deadline management skills built over three years.

Future Pathway Guidance

Career counselling, stream selection advice and exposure to opportunities beyond school to help students plan ahead with confidence.
