About Us

The Visionaries Behind Our Institution

The journey of Dr. Mar Theophilus School has been shaped by the vision, dedication and leadership of individuals who believed in the transformative power of education.

Our Patron

Late H.G. Dr. Philipose Mar Theophilus

Late His Grace Dr. Philipose Mar Theophilus was a guiding inspiration behind the establishment of Dr. Mar Theophilus School. His vision of promoting quality education and nurturing young minds through knowledge, discipline and strong moral values laid the foundation for the institution. His belief in education as a means of empowerment continues to inspire the school’s philosophy and commitment to holistic development.

The school carries forward his vision with the aim of shaping confident, responsible and compassionate individuals.

Our Founder

H.G. Geevarghese Mar Coorilos

His Grace Geevarghese Mar Coorilos, Metropolitan of the Diocese of Bombay, serves as the Founder of Dr. Mar Theophilus School. With a deep commitment to education and the development of young minds, His Grace continues to provide guidance and leadership to the institution. His encouragement and vision strengthen the school’s pursuit of academic excellence, value-based education and holistic development, inspiring the school community to uphold its founding ideals.

Our Founder Director

Late Dr. C. J. Samuel

Late Dr. C. J. Samuel played a significant role in translating the vision of the institution into reality. As the Founder Director, he provided dedicated leadership and guidance during the formative years of the school. His commitment to academic excellence, systematic administration and the overall development of students helped establish a strong foundation for the institution.

His contribution remains an integral part of the school’s journey and legacy.

Today, Dr. Mar Theophilus School continues to move forward with the vision and ideals of its founders. With an unwavering commitment to academic excellence, holistic development and value-based education, the school strives to empower every learner to discover their potential, pursue their aspirations and contribute meaningfully to society.

Rooted in its vision, inspired by its legacy and driven by the possibilities of tomorrow, Dr. Mar Theophilus School continues its journey towards excellence.

Founder's message

A beacon of education

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome parents, students and well-wishers to Dr. Mar Theophilus School. Our vision is to build an institution that stands for educational excellence, character and compassion.

With the support of families, teachers and management, our students will continue to grow into thoughtful leaders and responsible citizens.

Vice President's message

Empowering children to lead with purpose

Quality education is the compass that helps young people navigate a changing world. True education develops wisdom, resilience, integrity and compassion beyond the classroom.

We remain committed to nurturing intellectual excellence alongside strong moral and spiritual values, so every learner can use their gifts for the good of others.

H.G. Geevarghese Mar Coorilos
