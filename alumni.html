Our Alumni

Once a Theophilian, Always a Theophilian

The alumni of Dr. Mar Theophilus School form a close-knit community bound by shared memories, common values and a lifelong connection to the school that shaped them. From engineering and medicine to arts, entrepreneurship and public service — our graduates are making meaningful contributions across diverse fields.

We actively celebrate our alumni community and encourage former students to stay connected, give back and inspire the next generation of Theophilites.

Dr. Pratima Mourya
2018 Batch

Life outside school is full of new experiences, challenges and dreams, but the foundation of it all begins at Dr. Mar Theophilus School. I cleared NEET-UG in 2020 and earned a place at Grant Government Medical College & Sir JJ Group of Hospitals, Mumbai, to pursue MBBS.

Having now cleared NEET-PG in August 2026, I look forward to beginning postgraduate training in Radiodiagnosis.

I am forever grateful to God, my parents, my school and my teachers for their guidance and support throughout this journey

I am grateful to my teachers for their guidance and encouragement. Dr. Mar Theophilus School will always be where my journey began

Avishkaar
2020 Batch

Science has always fascinated me and I feel fortunate to have turned that curiosity into my academic pursuit. I am currently pursuing my Master’s in Physics in Germany while working as a Research Assistant at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (AEI). My research involves improving waveform models for LISA, a space-based gravitational wave mission by NASA and ESA.

Dr. Mar Theophilus School holds a special place in my life, as it was where my interest in academics and scientific exploration began to take shape. The foundation, values, and encouragement I received from my teachers continue to inspire me as I explore new frontiers in physics and research.

I remain deeply grateful to my alma mater for being an important part of my growth, both as a student and as an individual.

Kanishk

Dr. Mar Theophilus School has been the foundation of my journey, shaping not just my academic abilities but also my values, discipline, and determination. The guidance of my teachers and the memories I made here will always remain an integral part of who I am today.

My message to all students is simple: Never lose sight of your dreams, stay committed to your purpose, and no matter how far you go, never forget where you started.

Aarush Prasad Khisti
